## Domain: learnimo.net + HTTPS

Good news: **Vercel provides HTTPS automatically** via Let's Encrypt — you don't need to configure SSL yourself. The moment you point your domain to Vercel and it verifies ownership, it provisions a certificate automatically.

Here's what you need to do at Locaweb:

### Step 1 — Add the domain on Vercel first

1. After you deploy the web on Vercel, go to your project → **Settings → Domains**
2. Add `learnimo.net` and `www.learnimo.net`
3. Vercel will show you the DNS records to set — it'll be one of these options:
   - **Option A (recommended for apex domain):** Two `A` records pointing to Vercel's IPs
   - **Option B:** A `CNAME` for `www` pointing to `cname.vercel-dns.com`

### Step 2 — Update DNS at Locaweb

In Locaweb's panel (using the same login you used to register the domain), go to **Zona de DNS** and add:

| Type | Name | Value |
|------|------|-------|
| `A` | `@` (apex/root) | `76.76.21.21` (Vercel's IP) |
| `CNAME` | `www` | `cname.vercel-dns.com` |

> Vercel will show the exact values — use theirs, not generic ones.

### Step 3 — Wait for DNS propagation

Takes 5–60 minutes typically. Once Vercel detects the DNS update, it auto-provisions the HTTPS certificate. You'll see a green checkmark on the domain in Vercel's dashboard.

### Also: click the ICANN confirmation email

You mentioned the ICANN email — **click that link first** before doing anything else. The domain won't fully activate until you confirm it.
